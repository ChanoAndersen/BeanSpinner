Yes, it says beans.. It should be more generalized, but that'll be version 2.

You can ban a bean, by adding the banned_beans parameter to the URL. Use semicolon as a separator.

Out of bean 1? Add banned_beans=1

Out of beans 2 and 4? Add banned_beans=2;4

Banning all beans, will just pick a random bean after a short delay. Only 100 attempt to find an unbanned bean, will be made.
